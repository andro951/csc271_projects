MTG Proxy Card Help

What is Bulk Proxy Forge?

Bulk Proxy Forge is designed to help prepare printable MTG proxy cards from a deck list. Create an entire deck at once instead of preparing every card individually.

How It Works

  1. Provide a link to your deck.
  2. Choose the look of your cards.
  3. Generate and review the cards.
  4. The cards will be sent to the printer website.
